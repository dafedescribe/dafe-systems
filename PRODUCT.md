# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Managing Directors & Operations Heads**: Industrial manufacturers, oilfield logistics companies, engineering equipment distributors, and technical trading firms looking to streamline quote turnaround, RFQs, and tender bids.
2. **Operations Managers & Workflow Leads**: Teams managing manual data entry across PDF drawings, supplier price catalogs, email attachments, and legacy ERP spreadsheets.
3. **Corporate Executives & Educators**: Organizations seeking applied AI & automation training that grounds staff in deterministic workflows (APIs, n8n, Python) without hype.

## Product Purpose

Establish Anthony Amalega as an independent technical systems builder and generate qualified workflow inquiries from industrial and operational businesses, while serving as a durable home for verified engineering case studies and technical writing.

## Positioning

Independent systems builder with an engineering degree (B.Eng Industrial Engineering) and live instruction experience who builds deterministic operational workflows for businesses where mistakes carry real financial consequences. Unlike SaaS templates or AI marketing agencies, every system keeps human judgment at the center while automating repetitive preparation.

## Operating Context

- Commercial industrial workflows: Inbound RFQs arriving as multi-page PDF scans, custom Excel spreadsheets, and unstructured email threads.
- Tender monitoring across public procurement boards and utility portals.
- Recurrent operational reporting involving hundreds of thousands to millions of data records.
- Live corporate and technical classroom training environments (e.g. AppClick Technology).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-intent commercial intake forms with WhatsApp fallbacks.
- Verified case studies only (no synthetic benchmark claims).
- Technical Catalogue architectural design system: Source Serif 4, Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono, paper canvas palette, and hard edges.
- No SaaS tropes, no generic marketing copy, no fake client logos, no fake statistics.

## Product Principles

1. **Automate preparation, keep judgment human**: High-value commercial commitments require experienced human sign-off; automation extracts, matches, and compiles.
2. **Deterministic over probabilistic**: Code and structured parsing come before LLMs; probabilistic tools handle unstructured variance only where necessary.
3. **Verified outcomes only**: Present only real systems, real constraints, and verified operational results.
4. **Catalogue clarity**: The interface functions as an engineering dossier and specification sheet rather than a consumer landing page.
