# Homepage persona simulation — 2026-09-21

## Test setup

- Surface: homepage at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844
- Evidence: browser snapshots and full-page screenshots with reduced motion enabled
- Decision being tested: can a first-time prospect identify Odafe, understand the work, trust the evidence, and reach a low-friction next step?
- Accuracy rule: organization marks communicate working context, not customer endorsement; prototypes remain labelled as prototypes.

## 1. Industrial managing director

**Situation:** Has an RFQ, tender, or quotation bottleneck and wants evidence that the builder understands commercial risk.

**First impression:** The headline names the operational problem before naming tools. The founder portrait and Ibadan/global line make the practice feel accountable rather than faceless.

**Trust checks:** Pass. Dangote and SR Construction are presented under “Experience across industrial, education & technical environments,” and the workflow diagram keeps engineering review and human sign-off visible.

**Likely objection:** “Are these live customer systems?” The evidence ledger answers this by displaying `PROTOTYPE` and `INTERNAL BUILD`; prototype outcomes are labelled “Target outcome.”

**Action clarity:** Pass. “Discuss a workflow” and “Show me the workflow” ask for the existing process, with no polished RFP required.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 2. Operations lead

**Situation:** Manages repeated copying, checking, spreadsheet updates, email attachments, and handoffs.

**First impression:** The page uses familiar inputs—PDFs, spreadsheets, inboxes, ERP/CRM—and shows how they move through extraction, review, and dispatch.

**Trust checks:** Pass. Each featured system exposes input, output, and outcome instead of relying on generic automation claims. The “software follows the work” position lowers fear of a forced platform replacement.

**Likely objection:** “Will this be maintainable after handover?” The operating principle and three-step working method answer this directly.

**Action clarity:** Pass. The prospect can start with the bottleneck rather than selecting a technology or preparing a specification.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 3. Training buyer

**Situation:** Needs practical AI and automation instruction for staff with mixed technical backgrounds.

**First impression:** AppClick appears near the top as technical instruction experience, and applied training is one of three clear entry points.

**Trust checks:** Pass. The page describes APIs, workflow logic, data handling, and responsible AI-assisted systems; it does not promise vague “AI transformation.” The CV adds curriculum design and project-based instruction.

**Likely objection:** “Is training secondary to consulting?” The dedicated training route and homepage section are easy to find, although industrial systems remain the deliberate primary positioning.

**Action clarity:** Pass. “Explore applied training” gives a direct route without competing with the primary workflow inquiry.

**Verdict:** PASS

## 4. International prospect

**Situation:** Encounters the site from outside Nigeria and needs location, availability, credibility, and communication clarity quickly.

**First impression:** “Based in Ibadan, Nigeria · Working globally” is visible in the first viewport. The founder portrait, named practice, English copy, manual language selector, CV, email, and WhatsApp create several verification and contact paths.

**Trust checks:** Pass. The identity is personal and specific, while the page avoids unsupported “global client” claims. The organization rail is framed as experience rather than endorsement.

**Likely objection:** “Can we collaborate remotely?” The global availability line answers the basic question; the contact flow asks for the real process and does not introduce a geography-specific barrier.

**Action clarity:** Pass. The same workflow inquiry works across locations, and the language selector remains user-controlled rather than changing content unexpectedly.

**Verdict:** PASS

## Shared findings

| Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Human identity is immediately visible | PASS | Named founder, supplied portrait, role, location |
| Logos increase trust without implying clients | PASS | Factual “Working Context” heading and role descriptors |
| Workflow evidence is concrete | PASS | Input/output/outcome ledger and workflow architecture |
| Prototype claims are distinguishable | PASS | Scope badges and “Target outcome” label |
| Global availability is explicit | PASS | First-viewport location line and contact options |
| Inquiry friction is low | PASS | Prospect describes the current process; no polished brief required |
| Graphics distract from credibility | PASS | Editorial rules, restrained amber accents, one portrait, no decorative illustration stack |
| Mobile route remains usable | PASS | Single-column hierarchy, readable CTAs, intact portrait/logo/evidence order |

No blocker appeared across two or more personas in this simulation.
